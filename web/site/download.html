Verify the download

Every release is signed with minisign. The public key is minisign.pub in the repository.

minisign -Vm selectDb-darwin-arm64.zip -P $(cat minisign.pub | tail -1)

Then what

Unzip, move it where you keep your apps, and open it. Getting started takes you from download to first query in about five minutes.
